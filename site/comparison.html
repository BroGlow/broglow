Three Lazarus reviews

ReviewConfirmed issuesAfter fixes
Product accuracyMixed-session settings did not affect the demo’s signal.Fixed; reviewer re-checked the shared calculation and regression.
CorrectnessDismissing sessions left the outer tray stale, including after all sessions were cleared.Fixed; tray follows the app without recreating dismissed sessions. Reviewer re-check passed.
Experience/accessibilityA failed 3D view stayed invisibly focusable; the photo fallback advertised brightness changes it could not display.Fixed; failed/loading model is inert, photo brightness is disabled, labels/controls recover on model success. Reviewer re-check passed.

Automated regression checks pass. Browser checks confirmed mixed-session priority, dismissal and the empty-session tray state. All three reviewers report no remaining confirmed findings.

Follow-up fix: clicking or keyboard-focusing either demo window now brings it forward without resetting sessions. The waiting-session Focus → Allow → return-to-BroGlow sequence was also checked in the browser.

AR and social previews are now restored. Supported phones can launch native AR; desktop visitors get a QR handoff on reachable hosted pages. Local addresses show a public-viewer option instead of an unusable QR. Open Graph/Twitter previews use our own hardware photo. Direct printable-model links are available in the optional-lamp FAQ and build guide.

Scope and evidence

Compared the current files and rendered pages at site/ and site/ (promoted homepage). Product behavior was checked against the shipped popover and project guides. Regression tests cover the new fixes, localization, theme choices, message validation, LED mapping, scroll progression and reduced motion.

The new app demo uses synthetic sessions. No native services, installers, physical hardware or phone AR were exercised. 3D failure and recovery paths were verified through automated tests. The new landing is now the production homepage source. This report does not establish a hosted deployment.

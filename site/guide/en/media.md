# Project images and photos

Use only our own media: CAD renders, screenshots, diagrams and photos of the
build. Keep third-party theme and library license notices intact.

Renders and the website's interactive 3D/AR preview come from the release
enclosure CAD with illustrative electronics. Label renders as renders; they do
not substitute for physical-fit results. The app's status icons are our own
([`assets/icons/`](../../../assets/icons/), generated by `render.py` there).

Keep the entire `models/broglow/preview/` directory ignored and untracked,
including renders, dimension diagrams and render recipes. Keep selected app and
site assets and reviewed validation data in Git.
[`scripts/check-generated-artifacts.py`](../../../scripts/check-generated-artifacts.py)
rejects tracked previews, CAD exports, archives and retired media.

## 3D and AR preview

The website shows the magnetic enclosure from `models/magnetic/`, with reference
magnets and electronics. Tracked outputs in `site/assets/`:

- `broglow.glb` and `broglow-model.json`: the assembled lamp
  (`models/magnetic/appearance.py`), used for the browser preview and phone AR;
- `assembly.glb` and `assembly-model.json`: the assembly explorer
  (`models/magnetic/exploded.py`);
- `broglow.png`: the poster and no-JavaScript fallback, a Working-state render of
  `broglow.glb`.

The JSON files record source hashes and bounds. Regenerate the outputs instead of
editing them: run [`scripts/export-web-model.py`](../../../scripts/export-web-model.py)
when the CAD changes (add `--exploded` for the assembly) and verify with `--check`.
Website builds serve the committed exports without rebuilding the enclosure. Use
the enclosure release package for printing. Commands and requirements:
[website README](../../README.md#interactive-3d-and-ar).

## Wanted photos and screenshots

- Main photo: the finished lamp from the front at a slight angle, USB cable visible.
- Open assembly: board, LED mounting and wire routing before the cover is fitted
  (power disconnected).
- Wiring close-up: unpowered board with readable GPIO labels, resistors, LED
  polarity and shared GND.
- App screenshot: the macOS menu-bar popover or Windows tray window with
  harmless example sessions.
- Lamps screenshot: the **Lamps** view with a connected lamp and brightness control.

## Capture and handoff

- Phone originals are enough. Use soft daylight and a plain background; keep LEDs
  visible without blowing out their colors. Avoid filters and portrait-mode blur.
- Send original full-resolution photos, ideally at least 2000 px on the long edge;
  JPG, PNG or HEIC is fine. Do not send screenshots of photos or messenger-compressed copies.
- Capture app screenshots as PNG at native resolution. Use example task names and
  remove private prompts, file paths, account details and device identifiers.
- No need to crop or add text; the maintainer prepares landscape and square crops.
- Note who took each photo and whether the hardware shown is a prototype or the
  final build. Remove location metadata from the web copy before publication.

Keep source originals outside the downloadable app bundle. Add only selected,
web-sized copies to `site/assets/` and list each under
[Our asset sources](../../README.md#local-downloads-and-factual-limits) in the
website README.
